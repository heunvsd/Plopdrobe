Instructions

Plopdrobe is made for the things you put down throughout the day. Two angled wooden poles, open metal curves and a circular base create places to drape, hang and place your belongings. Each part offers a simple point of contact, allowing familiar movements to guide how you use the object. Use the wooden poles for clothes and hats, and the open metal curves for bag handles or scarves. Add or remove items as you move through the day, letting their arrangement change with your routine. The object takes on a different shape with each thing you leave on it.

About

Plopdrobe is a lifestyle brand shaped by the everyday habit of putting things down. A coat draped over a chair, a bag beside the bed, belongings kept within reach—these familiar scenes are the starting point of our design. We create objects that give these gestures a place. Angled lines and open curves invite you to drape, hang and place your things with ease. As clothes and belongings are added, their folds, textures and overlapping shapes become part of the object, creating a composition that changes through daily use. Named after the brief sound of something landing, Plopdrobe finds its rhythm in these small, repeated actions. Sculptural when empty and personal when in use, each object holds a scene of everyday life.
